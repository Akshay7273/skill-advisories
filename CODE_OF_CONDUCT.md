# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `skill-advisories` a welcoming,
safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while focusing discussion on evidence and impact.
- Respect coordinated-disclosure boundaries and private security reports.
- Accept corrections and disagreements without personal attacks.
- Protect private information and never pressure reporters to reveal identities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misinformation, and publishing exploit material or private report details
without authorization are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `akshaykumar7273@gmail.com`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, close interactions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This code applies to repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions,
and project-related public or private communication.
